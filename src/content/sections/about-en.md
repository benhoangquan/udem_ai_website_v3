We are **UdeM AI**, a student-driven community at the **Université de Montréal** (DIRO / Pavillon André-Aisenstadt) dedicated to exploring specific and advanced topics in Artificial Intelligence.

Hands-on workshops. Outcome-driven research squads. Weekly paper distillations. We operate as an open student research incubator bridging undergraduate and graduate students with Montreal's AI ecosystem—preparing for machine learning competitions, participating in **CUCAI** (Canadian Undergraduate Conference on AI), and turning rigorous engineering into publishable work.

- **Weekly Sessions**: Fridays 12:45 – 13:45 @ AA-1207 (Pavillon André-Aisenstadt) & [Discord](https://discord.gg/2Ttnw8p2Hy)
- **Focus Tracks**: Model Quantization & Compression, Inference Serving & AI Infrastructure, Robotics & Reinforcement Learning, and Foundational Paper Distillation

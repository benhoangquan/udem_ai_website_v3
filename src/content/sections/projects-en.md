We are transitioning UdeM AI into a hands-on **student research lab**. Autonomous project squads form each semester around three core technical tracks:

1. **[TRACK 01] Model Quantization & Compression** — Running frontier open-weight models on constrained hardware with minimal perplexity or accuracy loss.
2. **[TRACK 02] Inference Serving & AI Systems** — High-throughput runtime optimization, KV-cache scheduling, and hardware-aware deployment pipelines.
3. **[TRACK 03] Robotics, RL & ML Competitions** — Applied reinforcement learning, imitation learning (DAgger / policy distillation), and international ML challenges aimed at workshop publications.

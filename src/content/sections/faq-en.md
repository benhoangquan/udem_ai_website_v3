**Q1: Who can join UdeM AI?**
Anyone! Whether you are an undergraduate or graduate student in Computer Science (DIRO), Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, Cognitive Science, or another discipline, you are welcome as long as you are curious and eager to build.

**Q2: When and where do you meet?**
We meet every **Friday from 12:45 to 13:45** at **Pavillon André-Aisenstadt (Room AA-1207)** at Université de Montréal. Announcements and room updates are posted in our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/2Ttnw8p2Hy).

**Q3: Do I need prior AI research experience?**
No prior publication experience is required to attend weekly workshops and paper distillations. For research project squads, basic Python and familiarity with PyTorch or linear algebra will help you hit the ground running.

**Q4: How can I access presentation slides and reading lists?**
All weekly slides (PDFs) and Paper of the Week links are published right here on the website under [presentations](/presentations) and [papers](/papers), as well as on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/2Ttnw8p2Hy).
